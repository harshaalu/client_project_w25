Counseling and Psychological Services

Mental Health and Wellness Support

At the University of Michigan, we take your mental health seriously. Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) offers a range of support options to help you manage stress, deal with personal issues, and enhance your overall well-being.

Individual Counseling

Meet one-on-one with a trained counselor to discuss your concerns in a confidential setting. To schedule an appointment, please visit our Make an Appointment page.

Group Therapy and Workshops

Join peer support groups such as Wolverine Support Network or attend workshops focusing on specific topics like stress management and mindfulness. We also have group therapy with dogs! Sign up today on Sessions to brighten up your day.
